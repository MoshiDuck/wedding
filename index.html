Questions & Answers

We've answered the most common questions below — reach out if you need more!

The ceremony takes place on Saturday, June 6, 2026 at 7:00 PM at The Baroness, located at 512 Conti Street, New Orleans, Louisiana in the French Quarter. Please arrive 15–20 minutes early to be seated comfortably before the ceremony begins.
The dress code is Black Tie Optional. Think floor-length gowns, velvet or silk cocktail dresses, tuxedos, or dark tailored suits. Our palette draws on deep brown-black, antique olive, dusty rose, and muted plum — wearing those tones is welcome but never required.
The French Quarter has several paid parking garages nearby along Decatur Street and at the Royal Sonesta. Street parking is limited on weekends. We highly recommend rideshares (Uber/Lyft) so you can fully enjoy the evening without worrying about parking!
Yes! Both the ceremony and reception will be held at The Baroness. After the ceremony, we'll celebrate with dinner, cocktails, dancing, and joy — all in the same beautiful space.
Plus ones are indicated on your invitation. If yours reads "and guest," please bring someone special! If you're unsure, reach out to us directly and we'll be happy to clarify.
We love your little ones! Children are welcome — please note them on your RSVP so we can arrange proper seating. If you prefer a night out without the kids, we completely understand too!
Please RSVP by May 1, 2026. You can RSVP right here on this website using the "RSVP Now" button on the Invitation tab. We need your response to finalize seating, catering, and all the beautiful details!
Absolutely! We'll have a full dinner service with cocktails, wine, and champagne for the toast. If you have dietary restrictions or allergies, please note them on your RSVP so our caterer can accommodate you.
New Orleans has incredible accommodations! Being in the French Quarter, you'll be surrounded by charming boutique hotels, historic B&Bs, and major brands — many within walking distance of The Baroness. Check back soon for a hotel block code, or feel free to book your own adventure in one of the world's most magical cities.
We are hosting an unplugged ceremony — please put phones and cameras away while we exchange our vows. We want everyone fully present for this sacred moment, and our photographer will capture every detail. After the ceremony, share everything! Tag us: #KamiAndGabriel2026 🌹
We're happy to help with anything at all! Reach out to us directly and we'll get back to you as soon as possible. We are so grateful you're celebrating with us — we cannot wait to see you on June 6th! 🌹
